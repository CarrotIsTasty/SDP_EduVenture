Topic 6: Feasibility Study

Feasibility Study

Feasibility Study is use to determine whether a project has a reasonable chance of success.

Type of Feasibility Study

Technical Feasibility

Process of determining whether the organization has the technology resources to develop or purchase, install, and operate the system. For example,

Q: Is the proposed technology or solution practical?
A: Yes, similar system is widely used in other universities.


Operational Feasibility

The process of assessing the degree to which a proposed system solves business problems or takes advantage of business opportunities.

Refers to a system that users will accept and use effectively to support business objectives.

The PIECES framework can help identify operational problems to be solved and their urgency.

PIECES Framework

Performance

1. Does the current mode of operation provide adequate throughput and response time?
2. Is the new system going to be more efficient and high output?

Ans: Yes, the new system is going to be THREE times faster and it its going to produce TWO times more output compared to the old



Information

1. Does the current mode provide end users and managers with timely, pertinent, accurate and usefully formatted information?

Ans: No, the new system has better search and results, generate automatic reports, etc.



Economy

1. Does the current mode of operation provide cost-effective information services to the business?
2. Could there be a reduction in costs and / or an increase in benefits?
3. Does the proposed system save more cost then older system?

Ans: Yes, the new system will allow 20% reduction in maintenance cost. The new system will have less problems to fix.



Control

1. Does current mode of operation offer effective controls to protect against fraud?
2. Does current mode of operation guarantee accuracy and security of data and information?

Ans: No, the new system is built with latest firewall. The new system have special access to managers to control domain functions.



Efficiency

1. Does the current mode of operation make maximum use of available resources, including people, time, flow of forms?
2. Do you need to hire / let-go people for the new system?
3. Do we have enough time to print daily report like before?

Ans: No, the new system need more people to maintain.



Services

1. Does the current mode of operation provide reliable service?
2. Is it flexible and expandable?

Ans: Yes, the new system can be easily upgraded, added more applications.


Schedule Feasibility

The process of assessing the degree to which the potential time frame and completion dates for all major activities within a project meet organizational deadlines and constraints for affecting change.

Q: How much time is given for the project by customer?
A: Estimated 6 weeks.


Economic Feasibility

To concludes if the project can be done within the given budget and to determine the cost and benefit (including profit) from the project.

Q: Does the organization have adequate cash flow to fund the project during the development period?
A: Yes, the organization have enough cash flow during the development period.
